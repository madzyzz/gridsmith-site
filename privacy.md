# GridSmith Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

GridSmith sells digital spreadsheet templates through our Etsy shop, [GridSmithProducts](https://www.etsy.com/shop/GridSmithProducts). This page explains how we handle data in our shop and in the small tools we use to share our products on social media.

## Purchases

All orders and payments are handled by Etsy. We only see the order details Etsy shares with sellers, and we use them only to deliver your order and answer your questions. Etsy's own privacy policy applies to everything you do on Etsy: [etsy.com/legal/privacy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/privacy).

## Our social media tools

We use the official APIs of Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook and Threads only to publish posts about our own products to our own business accounts. These tools sign in only to accounts owned by GridSmith. They do not collect, store or sell data about other users, followers or visitors. Access keys are kept private and used for nothing else.

## The spreadsheets

Our spreadsheets run in your own copy of Google Sheets or Excel. Anything you type into them stays in your file; we never receive it.

## Your rights and contact

Questions, or want us to delete something we hold about you? Send us a message through Etsy or email [gridsmithproducts@gmail.com](mailto:gridsmithproducts@gmail.com). We reply within a few days.
